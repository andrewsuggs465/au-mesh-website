The Meshtastic guide is meant to serve as a brief introduction for students to
quickly get caught up to speed and build a node. The
[Meshtastic Docs](https://meshtastic.org/) should be used as a primary reference.

## Sections

- **[Meshtastic](/documentation/meshtastic/intro/)** — from "what is Meshtastic?"
  through building, flashing, and configuring your own node.
- **[Encryption](/documentation/encryption/encryption/)** — how encryption works
  and why it matters for mesh networks.
