## Sponsors

Sponsorship goes straight into the event. The club takes no cut and spends
nothing on overhead.

- **Hardware.** Microcontrollers, sensors, radios, and the consumables sixty
  students burn through in a weekend.
- **Food.** Five meals and overnight coffee, since teams stay in the building
  overnight.
- **Prizes.** The award budget for the general competition and the named
  sponsor awards.
- **Operations.** Makerspace staffing for after-hours access, printing,
  signage, and shirts.

The event is student-led. Teams pick their own projects, so sponsor awards are
written as goals to hit, such as best use of a given board or radio, rather
than as assigned builds.

### In kind

In-kind support counts toward a tier at fair market value, and for a hardware
event it is often worth more to us than cash.

- Development boards, modules, and sensors
- 3D printing filament
- Cloud credits and API access
- Test equipment on loan for the weekend
- Catering, or engineers willing to mentor for a few hours

Pick a tier and we will send a one-page agreement.
