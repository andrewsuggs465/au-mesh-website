Up to 60 Auburn Engineering students dedicate a weekend to designing,
building, testing, and troubleshooting hardware projects before demonstrating
their solutions on Sunday. Sponsors support the event by funding components
and may interact directly with student teams, providing guidance, mentorship,
and industry expertise.
