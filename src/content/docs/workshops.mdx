Hands-on sessions where you actually build something. Workshops are scheduled
throughout the semester — watch the [Events](/events/) page and
[Discord](https://discord.gg/PmmSCSw7Dn) for dates and signups.

:::note[How workshops run]
Workshops are kept small — around **10 people per session**, led by one officer
and one experienced club member. Instead of one 20-person workshop we run **two
10-person sessions**, and leaders rotate between workshops.
:::

## Beginner Workshops

No experience needed — these are designed as a first contact with the tools.
